Shelf — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Applies to the Shelf browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox and others) and the Shelf website.

The short version: Shelf has no accounts, no server, no analytics and no ads. Your apps stay in your browser.

What Shelf stores

The sites you save (address, name and logo), your folders, your to-do lists (text, notes, dates and reminders) and your settings. These are stored:

  • On your device, in the browser's extension storage. On the website, they are in IndexedDB.
  • In your browser account, only if Sync Across Devices is on. The browser (for example Chrome Sync or Firefox Sync) handles this copy under its own privacy policy. Shelf never sees your account.

Uploaded logos and a wallpaper photo stay on the device where you added them.

What Shelf sends over the internet

  • Opening an app simply opens that website, as clicking a link would.
  • Logos. To show a site's logo, Shelf loads the icon from the site itself, or from Google's public favicon service (t2.gstatic.com, www.google.com/s2/favicons). The request contains the address of the site. Logos you upload are never sent anywhere.

Nothing is ever sent to the developer. Shelf contains no tracking or analytics code, and it doesn't load scripts from the internet.

Permissions (extension)

Permission Why
Storage and unlimited storage Saves your apps, backups and settings on your device.
Active tab When you open Shelf, it reads the current tab's address, title and icon to offer "Add this site". Nothing is stored unless you add it.
Context menus Adds "Add Page to Shelf" and "Add to To-Do" to the right-click menu. When you use "Add to To-Do", the selected text and the page address are saved as a to-do on your device.
Alarms Schedules the reminders you set on to-dos.
Notifications (optional) Shows your reminders. Asked for the first time you set one.
Bookmarks (optional) Used only while you import bookmarks. Shelf then gives the permission back.

Your control

  • Export Backup gives you everything in one file.
  • Reset Everything removes it. Uninstalling the extension deletes its local data. Turning off Sync stops the account copy.
  • Shelf does not sell, share or transfer data, and it doesn't use data for advertising, credit or any purpose other than being your launcher.
